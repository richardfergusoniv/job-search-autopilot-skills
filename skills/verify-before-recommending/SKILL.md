# Verify Before Recommending

## Fetch the full official posting first

- Fetch the full job description text from the employer's official careers page or official ATS before a role appears in any recommendation or apply list presented to the user.
- Treat sweep output, aggregator pages, and alert emails as discovery leads only. Keep unverified leads in a separate "not yet verified" list that is never mixed into the recommended list.
- Confirm liveness on the official source during the same check. A role the official board no longer lists is a drop, not a recommendation.

## Check pay from the official text only

- Scan the full official job description, body and footer, for the disclosed pay range before stating any pay figure. Many jurisdictions require employers above a size threshold to print the pay scale in the posting, so a range missing from the summary fields is usually a retrieval failure, not a non-disclosure; it is often sitting in the footer.
- Never source a pay figure from an aggregator, a job alert email, or a search snippet. Say plainly when the official posting discloses no range instead of filling the gap from another source.
- Apply the user's pay floor gate before any other fit rule. No other consideration (a famous employer, a level adjustment) outranks the pay gate.

## Check craft against the top requirements

- Read the posting's top three requirements and map each one to a real analog in the user's record, judging the actual verbs and duties the role performs rather than its title or industry label.
- Name a gap by craft when the posting's core work differs from the user's work (for example, execution-side work against investor-side evidence), instead of framing it as a topic or sector gap.
- Hold the role for the user's call instead of recommending it when at least two of the top three requirements have no honest analog, or when the posting carries a material condition with no standing answer in the user's profile (heavy travel, a clearance, an on-site requirement outside their rules).

## Present the verification basis

- State the official posting link, the pay range and where in the official text it was found, and the top-three requirement mapping beside every recommended role.
- Record any role that fails pay, liveness, or craft verification as a drop or a hold with the specific evidence, so a later stage never has to reverse a recommendation.
- Recommend fewer roles with a stated verification basis rather than a longer list that still contains unverified leads.
