# Cover Letter Writer

## Gather the material

- Read the full posting text, the user's master resume as ground truth, and the role's application folder before drafting.
- Check the application form for a cover letter upload field. Write a letter whenever the form allows one and the pipeline calls for one. When the form has no letter field but requires a why-us free-text answer, the approved letter text serves as that answer.
- Use only facts traceable to the master resume or the user's standing application answers. Never invent projects, metrics, or tools.

## Draft the letter

- Mirror the posting's language in the opening and connect the user's most relevant real experience directly to the role's primary need.
- Build the body from specific mechanics: one story of a root cause found and fixed, with a lasting improvement, adapted to the posting's domain.
- Close with a confident day-one action in the applicant's own voice.
- Keep the letter under 330 words unless the form states another limit.
- Avoid generic claims (hard worker, detail oriented, team player) unless a concrete fact carries them.
- If a reframing playbook entry exists for this role family (rejection-review skill), use it, so the letter names the background-to-role translation explicitly instead of hoping the reader makes the leap.

## Write like a person

Run this pass over the final text:

- No em dashes. Use commas, periods, or a restructure.
- Cut stock AI phrasing: "delve," "leverage" as a verb for ordinary use, "furthermore," "moreover," "in today's fast-paced world," "passionate about," "thrilled to apply," "I am writing to express."
- Vary sentence length. Short sentences are fine. Fragments, sparingly, are fine.
- Every sentence should contain something specific to this user, this company, or this role. A sentence that could be pasted into any other letter gets cut or made specific.

## Review before submission

- Run a debate review with three roles: an advocate (the letter's best case), a devil's advocate (every weak, generic, or unsupported line), and a judge. Score JD coverage, specificity, unsupported claims, and whether a hiring manager for this role would be convinced.
- Revise against the top objections and repeat until both critics score at least 8 out of 10, or three rounds pass.
- Check company specificity separately: the letter could only be for this company; it does not repeat resume bullets; it contains no generic flattery.
- In the pipeline, the dedicated cover letter critic pass (job-search-pipeline skill) still runs after this; this skill's debate is the drafting-stage check, not a substitute.

## Review a letter on its own

- When the user asks only for a review: a proofreader pass (grammar, typos, consistency, formatting) and a substantive pass (narrative arc, personalization, qualifications, tone, conciseness).
- Report only; do not edit the source letter during a standalone review.
- Present one unified report with the lower of the two scores, critical issues first, then major and minor, each with a specific fix, in priority order.
